About Me

Security-driven, curiosity-led

I'm a BCA graduate with a deep interest in Cybersecurity, Ethical Hacking, and Network Security, currently interning at Infotact Solutions, Bengaluru. My interest in security started with a genuine curiosity about how systems work — and how they can be broken and made stronger.

My internship at Kerala Police Cyberdome gave me rare, first-hand exposure to cybercrime investigation — working with digital forensics tools, OSINT techniques, Tableau Forensics, hash verification (SHA-1 & MD5), CDR/IPDR analysis, and the investigation of real cybercrime cases. My goal is a career in Security Operations (SOC), Network Security, or Ethical Hacking.
